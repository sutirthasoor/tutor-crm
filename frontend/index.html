Step-by-Step Registration Process
Step 1 Tutor Registration Entry

Tutors register through multiple channels. System prevents fake/duplicate accounts.

Status: Contact Verified

Step 2 Personal Information Collection

Tutor provides: Full Name, Gender, Date of Birth, Photo, Address, Languages Known

CRM Validates: Mandatory fields, Age check (18+)

Status: Personal Info Completed

Step 3 Academic & Teaching Profile

Tutor enters academic details, subjects, boards, classes, experience, teaching mode, expected fees, availability.

Status: Teaching Profile Completed

Step 4 Document Upload Section

Tutor uploads: ID proof, Address proof, Qualification certificates, Marksheets, Resume.

CRM Functions: Check missing documents, Upload timestamp

Status: Documents Submitted

Step 5 Internal Verification Workflow

Admin verifies documents, conducts telephonic/video verification, checks communication skills, subject knowledge, experience.

Possible Outcomes: Approved, Rejected, Needs Correction

Status: Under Verification

Step 6 Approval Stage

If approved: Assign Tutor ID, Set status to Active, Add to automated matching system

If rejected: Send rejection reason with option to reapply

Status: Tutor Active & Ready

Matching Algorithm

The system filters tutors based on:

  • Location Proximity - Within 5km radius
  • Subject Expertise - Exact match preferred
  • Education Qualification - Relevant degree
  • Experience Level - Minimum 1 year preferred
  • Availability - Matches student timing
  • Expected Fees - Within student budget
Matching Score

Tutors are ranked by matching score (0-100%) based on how well they match the requirement.
